If NextBot is not responding, first check your internet connection and verify the service status at status.nextbot.example.com. Common issues include expired API keys, which can be regenerated from the dashboard under Settings then API Keys. If the bot gives incorrect answers, retrain it by uploading updated documents in the Training section. Response times over 5 seconds usually indicate high server load and resolve automatically. For billing issues, contact support at help@nextbot.example.com.
