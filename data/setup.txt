To set up NextBot, first create an account at nextbot.example.com. After signing in, click Create New Bot and choose your industry template. NextBot offers templates for retail, healthcare, restaurants, and professional services. Upload your FAQ document or product catalog so NextBot can learn your business details. The initial training takes about 10 minutes. Once training is complete, you can test your bot using the built-in chat simulator before deploying it to your website.
